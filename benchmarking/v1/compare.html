How to read this comparison

Maps share identical bounds, room labels, and colors. Visit count shows recorded visits to each tile; Share of visits divides each tile’s count by that run’s total visits, making different run lengths easier to compare. Colors use a shared logarithmic scale.

Visits include action endpoints and exact intermediate positions when recorded, including slides and punches. Height is collapsed onto the world map. Empty tiles have no recorded visits. These are exploration heatmaps, not terrain maps. Refresh to update an active run.

Imported runs are saved heatmap snapshots from the older leaderboard, with their original model and tool settings. They count the initial position and action endpoints; intermediate paths were not recorded. They remain available when the source site is offline.
