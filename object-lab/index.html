How it works
Use WASD or the camera pad to look around. In Play room, use the arrow keys or movement pad to move. Undo goes back one move; Reset restores your setup.
The text view uses the benchmark’s actual renderer. Each tile has a 4 × 4 character face; tilting changes how much of its top and side you see. Character size stays fixed.
Actors can hide buttons and other fixtures. Retracted orange walls reveal the surface covering them. Clone and weightless-body symbols can change with the identities present in a room.
